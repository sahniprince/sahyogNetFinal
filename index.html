The problem

Local services are broken on both sides

Customers can't verify who they are hiring. Skilled workers can't be found. Cooperatives have no visibility into demand.

🔍

Trust deficit

Customers struggle to find trustworthy skilled workers. Ratings alone don't prove identity or skill.

🧩

Fragmented discovery

Worker discovery is scattered across phone numbers, WhatsApp groups and informal contacts.

📵

Zero digital visibility

Skilled workers have no verified digital profile and lose work to whoever is called first.

📊

No demand intelligence

Cooperatives lack centralized service and demand data to plan training and deployment.

Key features

Built for trust, not just listings

◎

Smart Worker Matching

A weighted match score across skill, distance, rating, availability, response rate, completion rate and experience — with a transparent “Why this worker?” breakdown.

✓

Skill Verification Passport

A cooperative-issued digital passport listing each verified skill, certificate and verification level — portable across the network.

◉

Smart Service Radius

Starts searching within 3 km. If suitable verified workers are insufficient, the radius expands automatically to 5 km and tells the customer.

⇄

Backup Worker

If a worker does not respond, verified alternatives are surfaced instantly with match percentage, rating and distance — the booking is never left hanging.

★

Trust Score

Rating measures customer satisfaction. Trust Score measures platform reliability — identity, skill verification, experience, completion and service quality.

▲

Demand Intelligence

Completed jobs aggregate into zone-level demand heatmaps, skill gap analysis and training recommendations for cooperatives and federations.

Service categories

Real trades, real local demand

Categories are managed by cooperatives and mapped to verified skill sets.

Trust & verification

Verification is issued by cooperatives, not self-declared

A worker cannot simply tick “verified”. Cooperative administrators review identity documents, trade certificates and skill demonstrations before a skill appears on the passport.

  • ✓ Identity verification against cooperative membership records
  • ✓ Skill-wise verification, not blanket approval
  • ✓ Certificate and training credential upload
  • ✓ Periodic re-verification and suspension for poor performance
